Earlier, the VAE used an unweighted ELBO loss, where reconstruction and KL loss were combined as recon - kl. In the updated code, beta_recon and beta_kl are added to control the contribution of each term:
beta_recon * recon - beta_kl * kl
This gives better control over the trade-off between reconstruction quality and latent regularization. The model is also trained with L=10 samples earlier we were taking default L=1 sample, so the ELBO is averaged over 10 latent samples, giving a more stable loss estimate.
